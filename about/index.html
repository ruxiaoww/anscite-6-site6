About

Anscite is a company that provides generative engine optimization (GEO) services, making a brand appear in AI answers. This site focuses on the self-service account, one of the ways clients work with Anscite.

Who we are

Anscite makes a brand appear in AI answers. The foundation of this work is a brand fact library that records company information, product parameters, qualifications, cases, and real data. Through the self-service account, your team can use this fact library to generate and publish content and to probe AI engines.

For clients with their own content team, Anscite offers a self-service account that provides access to the same backend system. The account supports multiple brands in parallel, each with its own site and fact library. Clients can use their own model Key and deployment account, with costs settled directly with the upstream provider.

The self-service account has four role levels: super admin, admin, member, and Read-only. The Read-only Role can view all reports and content but is blocked from any write operations. Clients can run probe reports at any time to see how AI engines answer questions about their brand.

Anscite offers a Free First Probe Round to see how AI currently answers questions about a brand. For teams that prefer not to operate the platform themselves, Anscite also provides a managed service where the client provides brand materials and Anscite handles the rest, including periodic probe reports.

How we work

  1. Build the fact library

    Record company information, product parameters, qualifications, cases, and real data in a brand fact library.

  2. Mine real questions

    Mine real long-tail keywords from Baidu and Bing and build a list of questions customers would ask AI.

  3. Generate and review content

    Generate content based on the fact library and review each piece manually.

  4. Publish on independent sites

    Publish content as independent sites with built-in search engine and AI crawling optimization, supporting one-click publishing and binding of independent domains.

  5. Probe and report

    Probe multiple AI engines with the same set of questions and produce reports on mention rate, citation rate, ranking when mentioned, and which competitors are mentioned.
