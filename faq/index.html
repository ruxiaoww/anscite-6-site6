FAQ

What is the self-serve GEO platform?
It is a self-service account from Anscite that lets clients with their own content team use the same backend system. It supports multiple brands in parallel, each with its own site and fact library.
Can I use my own model Key and deployment account?
Yes. Anscite's self-service account allows clients to use their own model Key and deployment account, with costs settled directly with the upstream provider.
What role levels are available?
The self-service account has four role levels: super admin, admin, member, and Read-only Role. The Read-only Role can view all reports and content but is blocked from any write operations.
Can I run probe reports whenever I want?
Yes. Anscite's self-service account allows clients to run probe reports at any time. The reports cover mention rate, citation rate, ranking when mentioned, and which competitors are mentioned.
Is there a Free First Probe Round?
Anscite offers a Free First Probe Round to see how AI currently answers questions about a brand.
How do I contact Anscite?
You can reach Anscite at biz@anscite.com.
